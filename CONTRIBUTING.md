# Community Ethos & Workshop Guidelines

In this sandbox, everyone is welcome as their authentic, eccentric creative self.

### How We Critique
- **Focus on the craft:** Pacing, metaphor, thematic weight, voice, and emotional punch.
- **Workflow neutrality:** Never scorn a creator for their tools. Whether a line was sparked by a neural net, a fountain pen, or a 3:00 AM dream journal, critique what is on the page.
- **No commodity slop:** We do not host spam generated to exploit algorithms. Every piece must carry intentional thought, personal curiosity, or genuine inquiry.

### Submitting Work
1. Fork the repo.
2. Add your piece under `archives/essays/` or `archives/fragments/`.
3. Open a Pull Request with a short note on what kind of feedback you’re looking for (or file an Issue using the Workshop template).
