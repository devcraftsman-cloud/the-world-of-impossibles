# The World of Impossibles 🌌✒️
> *"Hey, you, don't tell me there's no hope at all. Together we stand, divided we fall."*

Welcome to **The World of Impossibles**—an open creative collective and repository for writers, philosophers, idealists, and theorists exploring the frontier of hybrid human-machine imagination.

We reject the stigma around modern creative instruments. We do not police the loom; we honor the weave.

## The Core Tenets
1. **The Instrument, Not the Ghostwriter:** We bend code like an electric synthesizer. The machine amplifies; the human directs.
2. **Radical Creative Sovereignty:** Taste is personal. If a piece isn't your style, don't wear it—but never burn the loom.
3. **The Human in the Loop:** Syntax without lived mortality is hollow. We celebrate the seams, the friction, and the human fingerprints.
4. **Originality Over Pedigree:** Plagiarism of another human's voice is the only cardinal sin. Stand behind your own mythologies.
